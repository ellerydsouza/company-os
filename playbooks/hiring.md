# Playbook: Hiring

**Trigger:** A decision to open a role (for the first hires, that decision
itself deserves an RFD — a hire is among the least reversible things a
startup does).
**Owner:** Hiring manager for the role (founder until delegated)
**Last verified:** 2026-08-06 (initial draft — revise after the first real loop)

## Before you start

- Prerequisite: a published compensation philosophy RFD. **No offers before
  it exists** — negotiating comp ad hoc creates permanent fairness debt.
- Access: ATS or the hiring spreadsheet/board, careers page, scheduling tool.

## Steps

1. **Write the role one-pager** before sourcing anyone: mission of the role,
   first-90-days outcomes, must-have vs nice-to-have skills. If you can't
   write the outcomes, you're not ready to hire for it.
2. **Build the scorecard** from the one-pager: 4–6 attributes, each with
   what "strong" evidence looks like. Every interviewer scores against these
   same attributes — this is the fairness and signal-quality core of the
   whole loop.
3. **Design the loop** and assign attributes to stages so each is covered
   twice and no interview is redundant. Default shape:
   - Screen (30 min) — mutual fit, motivation, comp-range check said out loud
   - Craft interview — a realistic work sample or deep dive, never puzzles
   - Loop interviews (2–3) — remaining scorecard attributes
   - Founder conversation — values, trajectory, their questions
4. **Source and respond.** Every applicant gets a response; every
   interviewed candidate gets a decision within 3 business days of their
   last conversation. Speed is a competitive weapon and slowness is rude.
5. **Written scores before debrief.** Each interviewer files scores +
   evidence *before* seeing others' opinions. Debrief discusses divergence;
   the hiring manager decides — it is not a vote, and a lukewarm consensus
   "fine" loses to one strong advocate plus no vetoes.
6. **References (2 minimum, at least one manager)** before offer, not after.
   Ask "would you hire them again for this role?" and listen for the pause.
7. **Offer call within 24 hours of the decision**, terms per the comp
   philosophy — enthusiasm, clear numbers, a deadline that's generous but
   real. Follow with the written offer the same day.
8. **On acceptance:** record the grant for the cap table, close the role,
   send rejections to remaining candidates personally, and hand off to
   [onboarding](../handbook/onboarding.md) (pre-day-1 checklist starts now).

## Escalation

- Any interviewer raises a values/integrity concern: hiring manager + founder
  decide together before proceeding — a skills gap is coachable, this isn't.
- Candidate negotiates outside the comp philosophy's bounds: escalate to
  founder; the answer that preserves the system is usually no.

## Done when

- Signed offer recorded, cap table updated, onboarding pre-day-1 checklist
  started, all other candidates answered.
- [ ] If any step was wrong, missing, or confusing: open a PR fixing this
  playbook before you close the role.
