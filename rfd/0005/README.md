# RFD 0005: Compensation philosophy

> **State: ideation.** This RFD scopes the decision and its options; it does
> not yet make it. It must reach `published` **before the first offer goes
> out** — the [hiring playbook](../../playbooks/hiring.md) hard-blocks on it.

## Problem

Without a written compensation philosophy, every offer is a one-off
negotiation. That is slow, systematically favors confident negotiators over
good candidates, and creates fairness debt that surfaces exactly when the
team is big enough for people to compare notes. The philosophy must exist
*before* the pressure of a live candidate, not during it.

## Decisions this RFD must make

1. **Positioning:** what percentile of market do we pay (e.g. 50th cash /
   aggressive equity, or 75th cash / standard equity)? Against which market —
   local, national-remote, or SF-benchmarked?
2. **Structure:** bands per role/level with placement rules, or a formula
   (à la GitLab/Buffer)? How much negotiation is allowed — the fairness-
   preserving answer is "little to none," which only works if initial offers
   are strong.
3. **Equity:** option pool size, grant sizing by stage/role, vesting
   schedule (4-year/1-cliff is default — deviate deliberately or not at
   all), early-exercise allowed?, post-departure exercise window (90 days
   vs extended — this one is a values statement).
4. **Refreshes and raises:** when are comp levels revisited (the annual comp
   ritual on the [operating cadence](../../handbook/operating-cadence.md)),
   and what triggers an off-cycle change?
5. **Transparency:** are bands internally visible? (Deciding "yes" later is
   much harder than starting there.)

## Inputs to gather before drafting

- Benchmark data for the first 2–3 roles you expect to hire (levels.fyi,
  Pave/Carta reports, peer founders).
- Current option pool size and dilution constraints from the cap table.
- A stance on remote geo-adjustment (single band vs location factors).

## Exit criteria for ideation

Someone claims authorship, fills the five decisions above with proposals,
and moves this to `discussion`. On publish: update the hiring playbook's
prerequisite link and add a `handbook/compensation.md` summarizing the
outcome (the RFD stays as the record of why).
