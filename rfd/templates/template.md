# RFD NNNN: Title

## Problem

What problem, question, or opportunity prompted this? Why does it matter now?
Write this so someone joining in a year understands the context.

## Proposal

The idea itself. Be concrete enough to disagree with. Diagrams, examples, and
strawman APIs/specs are welcome — precision beats polish.

## Alternatives considered

What else could we do, including "nothing"? Why is the proposal better?
(A rejected alternative written down is a future argument avoided.)

## Trade-offs and risks

What does this cost? What could go wrong? What would make us revisit it?

## Implementation / rollout

What changes when this is published? Which handbook pages or playbooks need
updating? Who does what, roughly when? What marks this RFD `committed`?

## Open questions

Things you genuinely don't know yet. Discussion should try to close these.
