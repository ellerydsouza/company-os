# RFD 0010: Materials-based hiring and public job descriptions

> **State: ideation.** Proposes upgrading the
> [hiring playbook](../../playbooks/hiring.md) from a conventional
> interview loop to Oxide-style materials-based evaluation
> ([RFD 3](https://rfd.shared.oxide.computer/rfd/0003),
> [RFD 146](https://rfd.shared.oxide.computer/rfd/0146)). Develop together
> with [RFD 0005](../0005/README.md) (compensation) — adjacent decisions.

## Problem

The current hiring playbook is a standard loop: screen → interviews →
debrief. Interview performance is a weak proxy for real capability ("work
is not a spelling bee"), it favors confident live performers over strong
quiet ones — the same failure mode [RFD 0008](../0008/README.md) fixes in
onboarding — and at founder scale, running multi-interviewer loops is the
most expensive possible evaluation per candidate.

## Proposal (to develop)

1. **Candidate materials before any interview**: work samples (code,
   designs, writing), plus written answers to a small set of reflective
   questions (proudest work, hardest challenge, why this company, values
   alignment). Written answers reveal depth that conversation improvises
   past — and they scale down: a founder can evaluate materials alone.
2. **Symmetry**: candidates who advance receive the interviewers' own
   materials (and RFD access) beforehand — peer conversation, not
   interrogation.
3. **Public, credential-free job descriptions**: responsibilities and
   day-to-day work, not degree/years requirements ("responsibilities are
   not qualifications"); ~five bullets max; no rockstar/110% language;
   externally recognizable titles.
4. **Boomerang rule** (from [Oxide RFD 618](https://rfd.shared.oxide.computer/rfd/0618)):
   departures are honorable; returners reapply through the identical
   process, no shortcuts.

## Decisions this RFD must make

- The reflective question set (Oxide uses ~8; likely fewer for us).
- How materials are scored against the scorecard the playbook already
  requires — materials replace *interviews*, not *rigor*.
- What interview time remains (Oxide: deep peer conversations after
  materials pass; likely 2–3 hours for us, not 9).
- Whether RFD access is granted to advancing candidates (transparency vs
  confidentiality of an early-stage repo).

## Exit criteria for ideation

Claimed and developed alongside RFD 0005; on publish, the hiring playbook
is rewritten around materials-first flow and a job-description template is
added.
