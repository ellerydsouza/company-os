# Vendor & contract register

Every vendor, subscription, and contract the company pays for — one row each.
Adopted via [RFD 0004](../rfd/0004/README.md).

**The rule:** signing up for anything (even a $10/month tool on a company
card) means adding the row in the same sitting. The register answers the
questions the books can't: *when does it renew, how long is the notice
period, who owns it, and how hard is it to leave?*

Reviewed: monthly financial review (spend deviations), annual
vendor/insurance ritual (every row re-justified or cancelled).

## Hardware-track additions

If/when the business ships a physical product, suppliers get their own
section — same table plus: **lead time**, **MOQ**, **second-source status**
(none / identified / qualified), and **tooling ownership** (who owns the
molds/fixtures — if it's them, that's lock-in). Single-sourced components
with no identified second source get flagged at every quarterly review.
